Privacy

421 Club · effective October 10, 2026

421 Club is a dice game. No account, no ads, no tracking. We don't collect any personal data: nothing is sent to a server of ours.

What stays on your phone

Your name, avatar or profile photo, settings, stats, achievements, game history, stakes and the cards you write in Party mode are stored only on your iPhone.

Your iCloud

If iCloud is turned on, this data is also copied to your private iCloud space so you can find it on your other devices. That space belongs to your Apple Account: we can't access it. You can turn it off in Settings › your name › iCloud.

Game Center

Online games, invites, the leaderboards (your ranking points among friends, in France and worldwide) and achievements use Game Center, a service run by Apple. During an online game, your Game Center nickname and your moves are sent to the other players at the table. This is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Playing around the same table

To play with several phones in the same room, the app uses the local network (Wi-Fi or Bluetooth) to send moves directly between phones. Nothing goes over the internet.

Photos

If you pick a profile photo, only that photo is read. The app can't see the rest of your library.

Chips and stakes

Chips are virtual and have no value. No money goes through the app. Stakes are just free text that stays on your phone.

Children

The app isn't meant for children and doesn't collect any data about them.

During a game with several phones (nearby or online), a small thumbnail of your profile photo is sent to the other players at the table so they can recognize you. It is not stored anywhere else. You can turn this off in Réglages › Montrer ma photo aux autres joueurs.

Deleting your data

In the app, Réglages › Mes données › Supprimer mes données erases your profile, photo, stats, chips, history and iCloud backup. Deleting the app also erases everything on the phone. iCloud copies can be deleted in Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › 421 Club.
